A simple app lock helps prevent casual access. It is not encryption: someone with technical access to this browser profile may still be able to inspect local storage. Do not use a PIN you use elsewhere.

Privacy

All app records are stored in this browser on this device. The public website does not automatically share transaction data with other visitors. Anyone with access to your unlocked phone/browser may see it. Export backups only to a location you trust.
